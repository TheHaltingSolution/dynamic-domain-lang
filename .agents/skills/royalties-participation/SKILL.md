# Royalties participation

When starting a new agreement, introduce the workflow in plain language. Say that you can turn their agreement into a readable calculation, show sample royalties for review, and compile a supported schedule after they approve it. Ask for the relevant agreement clauses or a description of the deal and any representative sample records. If they already supplied these, use them and ask for the unresolved choice with the greatest effect on the calculation.

## Elicit the rules and draft the schedule

- Tie each material rule to a clause, participant instruction, or explicitly labeled assumption. Gather the parties and recipients, eligible amounts and deductions, share or tier, period and currency, rounding, statements and balances carried forward as relevant. If participants disagree, record their alternatives and confirm who can approve the schedule. Ask about missing terms that can change money, recipients, timing or balances.
- State the admission rules for settlement inputs, including original record identity and which records belong to the period or payment batch. Ask what evidence supports these rules. Use synthetic examples when real records are unnecessary.
- Write proposed Readable SPL using [the supported forms](../../../docs/spl/readable-language.md), with the financial rules first and explicit policy links and technical bindings. Surface unresolved decisions, including any gap between the agreement and the supported language or certified profile. Keep a draft for review when a gap prevents certified execution. The language and compiler stay pinned for this workflow.

## Show the consequences and get approval

- Present the proposed SPL and linked policy, the evidence for each choice, and any alternatives still open. Work expected outcomes from original facts or a participant-supplied answer before comparing them to compiler output. Show a case that distinguishes ambiguous rules. See the [rounding walkthrough](references/rounding-review.md) for a concrete example.
- Preview the supported draft using the [command workflow](references/commands.md). Show the calculated amounts, recipient or scope, relevant review status and any returned state. Resolve discrepancies or open choices with the participant and preview the revised draft.
- Ask for explicit approval of the reviewed draft identity and its linked policy. A request for a sample, interest in an option, or a general instruction to proceed leaves the schedule at the review stage. Treat changed terms, source or policies as a fresh review that needs its own approval.

## Compile and use an approved schedule

- Record the participant's actual approval and compile through the supported certified route in the [command workflow](references/commands.md). Report the output location, approved revision, verified input domain and material limits. Describe generated amounts as calculated and unissued.
- For each later settlement, use the certified artifact with the new input and inspect the run receipt before reporting results. If verification, admission, execution or publication fails, surface the reported stage and stop consumption of that invocation. Keep input or package corrections separate from the prior approved contract.
