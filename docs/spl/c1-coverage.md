## What receives separate credit

A semantic row identifies an economic or interface discriminator. Its execution
fields distinguish source admission, canonical delivery, reference evaluation,
native IR, native-emitted Lean and separately Python-emitted Lean. The latter is
a separate source generator producing Lean, not a Python financial runtime.

Independent original-fact controls, boundary/invalid controls, actual serialized
state, native-IR audit observations and final artifact publication also need
their own evidence. A wrapper rejection earns wrapper credit; it does not prove
that SPL evaluated an invalid financial input. Agreement of emitted values and
errors does not measure emitted-target branch decisions. Successful source
programs do not establish exhaustive grammar/checker coverage.

A `qualified` row names retained bounded evidence. A `reported` row maps
previously recorded support without rerunning or recensusing it for this
catalog. Neither means that every option combination passed. A `partial`,
`pending`, `in_progress` or `gated` row preserves its remaining obligation.
A documentary alternative is not an unconditional additional implementation
requirement.

method. Equal outputs of other recipes on a particular fixture do not establish
