# Authorized dependent replay and prospective attribution

`SYNTHETIC-DEPENDENT-REPLAY-01@1` is an explicit bounded research assumption for
`C13.DEPENDENT_PERIOD_REPLAY` and `C13.PROSPECTIVE_PERIOD_ADJUSTMENT`. It is not a
contract interpretation, historical statement amendment, bank instruction or
professional approval. Currency is USD; no currency/FX extension is introduced.

## Original facts and monetary measures

The registry contains the complete original issued-period chain, actual original
receipt bases, original earning terms, immutable original issued artifacts,
separate actual bank payments, approved payment applications and a complete
historical payment population. It begins at an explicitly zero original opening.
All original issued amounts and cumulative meters are independently recalculated
from original bases and terms before they can serve as controls. An issued100
is not evidence that80 was paid; the separate bank/application/population proves
actual paid80. Original paid and original issued artifacts remain unchanged.

The simple case derives100 from original basis200 times original share.5, and90
from a separately approved replacement basis180 times that same original share.
The following period independently earns30 from its original basis60 times.5.
The original issued100/paid80 leaves original issued-unpaid20. Revised economic
entitlement is90+30=120; less actual paid80 gives outstanding40.

- Restatement supplies a calculated historical90/current30 view, with historical
  correction delta-10. Original issued100 is retained as the original artifact.
- Prospective attribution retains the original issued100 line and calculates
  current earning30 plus a separately authorized signed correction-10, giving
  current recognized20. It calculates the same total outstanding40.

The signed correction field is a delta, never a new90/120 total-value payment.
Both routes reconcile immutable issued-unpaid20 plus calculated unissued
current earnings30 plus authorized correction-10 to40. An adjustment is neither
issued nor paid by this oracle. Current participation cash settlement is an
explicitly evidenced complete-empty population; absent completeness is unknown,
and nonempty current cash requires a different policy. That observation does
not describe distributor sales-basis cash or erase historical bankpaid80.

## A true dependent-period calculation

The dependent case retains original issued P1=100 and P2=10. P1 uses original
basis200 and share.5. P2 has separate original basis60, with a cumulative-basis
marginal schedule:10% up to threshold220 and20% above it. From original opening
basis200, P2 earns20*.1+40*.2=10. Replacing P1 basis with180 changes P1 to90 and
P2's opening basis to180; P2 then earns40*.1+20*.2=8. Corrected cumulative basis
is240. Treating P2 as an independent unchanged10 would incorrectly leave20 due.

Actual paid80 remains80. Revised economic90+8 less80 gives outstanding18.
Original issued110 less paid80 remains the original issued-unpaid30 measure.
Restatement provides the calculated90/8 historical view. Prospective attribution
retains issued100/10 and proposes separate current signed lines-10/-2. Its
current economic earning is0 and current signed recognition is-12. The bridge
is original issued-unpaid30 plus calculated unissued earnings0 plus authorized
correction-12=18. A signed credit against this positive account balance is not a
bank refund or negative payment. The corrected basis meter240 governs future
original earning terms in both routes.

## Authorization, known views and lineage

Every replacement names one original issued receipt, its original basis line,
the same economic Date, a distinct replacement measurement line and a scoped
approved authority. The authority pins original issue ID, the selected route,
actual predecessor reference and the entire chronological dependent-period
suffix already closed at discovery. It explicitly applies revised basis to
future economics. New authorization cannot refer to an issue before that issue
existed. Replacement/request/authority observations are ordered and precede the
current exclusive exact Instant cutoff. Recorded approval dates precede as-of.

Original issue recomputation retains its original known cutoff and original
facts. New replacement evidence does not enter that old original known view.
The authorized revised economic view uses the current known replacement and
original terms, replaying each dependent cumulative meter from the original
opening. Future evidence, scope/role mismatches, broken dependency edges,
unknown original references and missing dependent periods reject atomically.
Receipt Date/sequence and correction recorded-Date/sequence are explicit complete
ordering keys. A tied key rejects; physical input order or lexical IDs cannot
choose monetary priority. State registry arrays are reported in original-ID order; their explicit
Date/sequence fields govern economics and every original field is retained.
Full source facts, exact number/time lexemes and valid
opaque Unicode Text identities remain unnormalized.

## State and scope

The neutral schema is `dependent-replay-neutral/1`; the state is
`dependent-replay-state/1`. State retains original registry, original current
frames, independently recomputed results, recognized period views, active
replacement originals and consumed request/original-receipt identities. Each
next opening must equal original-history replay and the supplied actual adjacent
predecessor summary. JSON-round-trip controls carry actual oracle closing state;
no supplied correction total or consumed-ID answer is trusted. A successor with
no new request recognizes no second-10/-12. Previous results are not mutated.
One authorized replacement per original receipt is selected; relabeling, splitting
or resubmitting the correction cannot manufacture another adjustment. Changing
route or immutable original registry after a close rejects.

This selected policy requires nonnegative account outstanding and no overpaid
original receipt after replay. It rejects an original overpayment instead of
flooring it or silently recovering against fresh earnings. Existing separately
authorized overpayment/offset policies remain separate. Upward revisions have
positive signed deltas, not automatic payment. Nonempty current cash, chained
re-replacements, changed original terms/registry, partial periods, insolvency,
external issue approval, bank execution and general concurrent commit are out
of scope.

Bounds are four total chronological ledger periods (original issued plus new
calculated frames), eight rows per ordinary table and32 evidence rows. These are
finite oracle admission limits, not native compilation/publication benchmarks.
The casepack explicitly supplies both mutually exclusive route factsets for
both scenarios. No SPL/compiler/adapter implementation was read to author these
controls. Research hashes identify the accepted0b91bc7 requirement/catalog
snapshot; later evidence updates do not repin these original requirements.
