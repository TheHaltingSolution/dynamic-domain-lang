# Owner decision: extensibility claim and native FX gate

23 November 2026. Decision pending; no gate has been waived.

Both RR-04 and LD03 found no qualified contract-required core residual after
considering faithful existing-language methods. Record-backed FX, complete-period
rational compounding and the bounded dated Quest method remain legitimate fits.
The requested MQP certificate is original human evidence, rather than a solved
financial input; the selected allocation/report can use ordinary SPL operations.
Versioned approved definitions likewise do not establish financial impossibility.

The current standing owner instruction therefore still gates native EUR/FX.
The reviewers propose this narrower, accurate research claim:

> Demonstrate that SPL can admit a versioned contract-calculation capability or
> stronger checked interface, with independently specified semantics, retained
> old-language alternatives, measured implementation/review effort and replay
> of the declared unchanged prior-contract corpus and its state. Do not claim
> the original kernel was financially incapable of every faithful encoding.
> After recording approval of this revised claim, allow the planned native
> currency/FX work to proceed under a versioned baseline and compatibility plan.

Choosing this proposal would explicitly replace the necessity prerequisite with
the measured extensibility/compatibility claim and authorize the planned native
currency work. It would not assert a previously inexpressible financial method,
waive evidence/rounding/type checks, repin issued artifacts, establish professional
approval or begin whole-compiler proof work. The finite FX backlog already names
USD/EUR, directed quote units, separate source/contract/payment currency,
date/evidence selection, rounding and unchanged-source/state replay acceptance.
Implementation must retain old tagged-record alternatives and preserve the old
baseline before adding that versioned interface.

Retaining the stronger necessity claim leaves `B-EXT-SELECTION` unresolved and
`B-C15-FX` gated. Ready non-FX engineering continues under either choice.
