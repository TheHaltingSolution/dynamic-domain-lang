## Adopted engineering decisions

- Retain the existing exact, nonempty, case-sensitive ASCII profile for declared
  `Id<Kind>` values, including its existing ASCII control/whitespace domain.
  Do not tighten it to printable IDs or normalize identities. Unicode `Text`,
  including existing Text-backed financial event IDs and tuple recognition
  keys, remains a different supported type. Outer generic scope strings are not
  universally declared `Id` values. This closes the design question; targeted
  documentation/backend alignment remains to execute.
- Minimum issuance thresholds apply to unissued entitlement plus new net
  earnings. Old issued arrears remain due on their original IDs. The existing
  three-period10/19/17 implementation remains valid: the final new candidate is
  27, not37. Correct the ambiguous backlog wording rather than changing this
  calculation.
- Add the missing separately named C10 burden/subtraction variation. A claimant
  entitlement100 remains100 while another agreement200 less an authorized30
  burden becomes170. Conserved transfer70/30 is a different method. Pin accrued
  versus paid measurement, scope/purpose/cap and exactly-once dependency identity;
  do not invent cash movement or impose global conservation on independent
  entitlements. The existing transfer item keeps its bounded acceptance; the
  broader C10 family remains partial until this second variation executes.
- The six deduction stage/base names are not six independently money-changing
  methods. Under the selected exact/no-rounding assumptions,
  `pre_basis_gross_fee` and `pre_share_gross_fee` have equal monetary results.
  Preserve stage/lineage observations, and classify monetary-equivalent mutants
  separately from a financial kill. Cap tests need identity/sequence controls,
  not only equal pooled totals.
- Keep complete declared-period views and explicit predecessor snapshots for
  C1. General raw incremental overlap/replacement intake stays unsupported and
  deferred; bounded restatement/replay need not depend on that future engine.
- No additional interest convention is required for the selected RR-04 slices.
  Keep accepted bounded full-period and Quest methods; reopen extra conventions
  only for a separately selected required case.
