## Start with an independent financial slice

The first ready feature is **B-C03-STAGES**, a reusable registered deduction and
fee library. Its first discriminator is already specified by C1: revenue 1,000,
cost 500 and share 80% gives 400 when sharing net, versus 300 when deducting
cost after sharing gross. Publish explicit stage names and a third fee/order
composition; retain eligibility, recognition date, base and ordering in the
readable policy. Supply original neutral facts and an independent oracle, then
canonical positive, zero, empty, equality and invalid cases through reference,
native IR and emitted Lean. A later slice adds cumulative deduction caps and
consumption identities. No new arithmetic primitive or outside research answer
is needed to begin this bounded synthetic library.

Run independent work alongside it: supplied withholding and minimum-payout
recipes (**B-C16-WITHHOLD**, **B-C12-THRESHOLD**), generated source/negative
cases, independent SV/LG oracles, and RR-03 workload/stage measurements. The
exact file ownership and shared interface contract must be agreed within the
implementation team before edits. Oracle authors start from neutral requirements
before inspecting the implementation they will check. Keep compiler/schema
sources and registrations stable during integration calculations.

"Ready" means the selected synthetic or engineering boundary is sufficient to
start; it does not establish a sourced contract interpretation. A listed
dependency must exist before final integration, even where independent policy,
fixture and oracle work can start sooner. Questions about a sourced alternative
do not block the separately named synthetic recipes or generic infrastructure.

## Sequence integration and performance work

1. Finish the ready validation increment and first staged-deduction/statement
   slices. Extend the executable feature matrix as methods become available;
   retain actual execution status separately from inspected stage declarations.
2. Add rounded allocation/cardinality and mixed-claim methods, then signed
   reversals/losses and transfer/obligation state. Add term/crossing/gate/calendar
   recipes as independent neutral controls become ready. Integrate every slice;
   do not accumulate an unverified library until the end.
3. Extend dependent-period replay and prospective offset compositions after
   obligation identities and predecessor boundaries are explicit. Keep original
   issued artifacts immutable and actual payments independent.
4. In parallel, measure 100, 1,000 and 10,000 mixed facts/events, separating direct
   native calculation, validation, comparison, audit generation and serialization.
   Record named hardware, inputs, source/build pins, cold/warm repetitions, CPU,
   latency, peak memory, output bytes, trace nodes/edges and failures. Count raw
   source rows, guard-counted facts/events, allocations and projected receipts
   separately.
5. Optimize only measured bottlenecks: indexes, stable sorting, graph/cache
   lookup, builders or repeated audit materialization. Existing archival trace
   packing happens after generation and is not a native-memory optimization.
   Preserve exact arithmetic, financial/error ordering and logical lineage with
   differential and independent controls.
6. Increase to the RR-03 100,000-record topology/fan-out probes only after smaller
   cohorts show headroom. Characterize independent scopes separately from a hot
   shared pool. Preserve the 100,001 rejection without consumable state. Publish
   a measured single-process envelope before making a bounded parallel-component
   claim.

RR-03's proposed warm p95 targets of 5/15/60 seconds and a ten-minute balanced
batch are targets to evaluate. They are not achieved service levels. A resource
guard or a raised limit does not establish capacity, and the existing compare
wrapper is not an isolated native benchmark.

## Close cross-cutting release obligations

The backlog explicitly includes readable multiple-party headings; finite named
schemas/enums and external relationships; evidence/approval labels; replacement
batch and raw-granularity lineage; non-FX reference series; generic CSV
roundtrips; report-date separation; raw rejection dispositions; uniform source
diagnostics; and interrupted-publication/stale-envelope protection. Supporting
authoring and schema improvements are distinguished from C1's mandatory economic
variants. Richer metadata is not a request to build an enterprise rights system.

Validation must grow beyond the current single generated template. Retain
well-typed boundary programs and deliberately invalid source, fabricated IR and
canonical packages as different rejection layers. Enumerate selected small
finite domains and constrained interactions with declared denominators. Use
metamorphic relations only where order, rounding and policy permit them.
Mutation reports distinguish killed, survived, invalid/equivalent and unexecuted
cases; the two existing Quest rounding mutations are only selected witnesses.

Maintain three evidence scopes: compiler path/rule mapping, actual generated
settlement decisions and domain method coverage. Current native traces describe
the interpreter. Add direct emitted-target instrumentation or a validated target
coverage mechanism before claiming target branch coverage. Preserve a simple
pure specification and a stated logical-trace relation when optimizing.

Each accepted variant needs an executable source and canonical neutral input,
visible selected policy and assumptions, independent material intermediates and
final values, positive/zero/empty/boundary/invalid witnesses, a targeted
composition, actual backend evidence and an updated ledger row. The eventual
finite release freeze also pins grammar/core/schema/diagnostics/trace/profile,
versions, compatibility exclusions, fixtures and expected results. Replay it
from a short isolated extraction using the pinned toolchain and this checkout's
Python imports. Run required build/smokes and the then-current full regression,
native suites and finite release witness commands; record real counts and exit
codes rather than adding historical cohorts together.

## Questions that apply to specific work

Eight items currently need an answer: the exact sourced retroactive repricing
boundary; sourced nonzero reserve terms; sourced dynamic gate history/crossing;
the genuinely required extension residual; ASCII versus NFC Unicode opaque IDs;
the authorized delta-intake overlap contract; actual professional review
dispositions; and a further selected partial-period/accrual convention. Each
question is attached to its own JSON item. Existing bounded variants can
continue, and no resolved Quest fixture questions are reopened.

The pre-FX gate remains binding. Research establishes contractual necessity;
design attempts faithful unchanged-language encodings. Existing FAME conversion,
complete-period LG compounding and bounded Quest certificates are legitimate
method fits. A missing builtin name, slower method or stronger native type is
not proof of financial inexpressibility. Only after identifying a justified
required residual should the team freeze a usable pre-extension compiler,
implement the smallest extension and replay compatibility. No candidate is
currently selected.

The public development target remains three to five sourced cases with at least
two materially different methods. SV and RQ-07 are one method. Existing Angel,
MQP and disclosed Quest work may be promoted only within its recorded policy,
source-domain and review limits; the target does not mean collecting three more
examples automatically. Professional approval requires an actual reviewer
disposition, and held-out case custody stays outside implementation prompts.

## Explicit limits

Full compiler/codec/trace proofs remain deferred. Do not introduce `sorry` or
custom axioms; existing toy and scalar proofs retain their stated scope.
Arbitrary recursive/host programs, unbounded cyclic cross-contract fixed points,
general optimization/IRR engines without selected cases, automatic legal/FX/tax
policy discovery and forecasting are not C1 requirements.

Vendor product parity, arbitrary vendor adapters, operational approvals,
signing/rights administration/portals, bank payment execution and distributed
transaction services are outside this finite calculation release. A named later
adapter or external capacity commitment needs its own permitted evidence.
Million-row and distributed service capacity remain unestablished. Completing
the engineering backlog cannot manufacture accountant approval, unseen holdout
performance or a proof that every possible SPL program is correct.
